# Ostrowski Dimensionless Reformulation (ODR)

**Status:** v2.0.1 — Published (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21754008)
**Date:** 2026-08-02
**Author:** Rowan Brad Quni-Gudzinas

## Overview

Systematic compilation and reformulation of fundamental physics equations that violate the Ostrowski Dimensionless Mandate (qnfo-core §0.7). The mandate requires all physics formulas in QNFO publications to use dimensionless Planck units (ℏ = c = G = k_B = 1), as dimensional formulations implicitly privilege the Archimedean (∞) completion of the rationals per Ostrowski's theorem (1916).

## Scope

Ten disciplines surveyed:
1. Quantum Mechanics
2. Thermodynamics & Statistical Mechanics
3. General Relativity & Gravitation
4. Quantum Field Theory
5. Cosmology
6. Electromagnetism
7. Atomic, Nuclear & Particle Physics
8. Condensed Matter Physics
9. Quantum Information
10. Fluid Dynamics & Plasma Physics
